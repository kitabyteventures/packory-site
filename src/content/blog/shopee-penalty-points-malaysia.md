## What Shopee penalty points actually are

Penalty points are not fines. Nobody sends you a bill. They are a performance record, and the restrictions attached to them are where the damage sits.

You can see yours in Seller Centre under Account Health, then My Penalty. On the Shopee App it sits under Me, then My Shop, then Shop Performance, then Account Health. Points appear within a day of being issued, and performance is reviewed weekly with updates landing on Mondays. One bad week can be followed by a spotless one, but the point earned in the bad week stays on record until the quarter resets.

## The four areas Shopee reviews

Shopee groups its minimum standards into four areas: listing violations, fulfilment of orders, customer service, and customer satisfaction. Listing problems cover prohibited items, counterfeit and intellectual property complaints, duplicate listings and misleading discounts. Fulfilment covers how orders leave your shop, customer service covers chat behaviour, and customer satisfaction covers ratings and how complaints are handled.

Most sellers who get blindsided are not breaking listing rules. They lose points in fulfilment.

## The fulfilment metrics that catch sellers out

Two rates do most of the damage, both measured over a rolling seven day window.

- Non-fulfilment rate (NFR): the share of your orders in the past seven days that were cancelled or returned.
- Late shipment rate (LSR): the share of your orders in the past seven days that were shipped late.

From 11 May 2026 Shopee also issues points for Fast Handover Rate, which tracks how quickly parcels get into the courier's hands after pickup. Check the current thresholds in your Seller Centre, not a screenshot from a Facebook group.

Read the NFR definition a second time. A return counts against the metric even when the buyer changed their mind, and even when the buyer says the parcel arrived empty. A cancellation logged by a buyer who picked the wrong variant counts too. What stays in your hands is whether you can show what left your table.

## What each tier costs your shop

Shopee revises the tier table from time to time, so confirm the numbers in your own Seller Centre.

- A shop at 6 points or more sits in Tier 2 and is excluded from marketing campaigns, so sale day slots are off the table.
- A deboosted shop gets a daily order limit. Once the limit is hit, the shop stops showing in buyer search and recommendations until it resets.
- Visibility returns when points drop below 3, or a successful appeal removes them.
- 15 points or more will freeze the account, and repeat counterfeit or intellectual property violations within six months can freeze it sooner.

One detail matters here. Shopee's own articles word the quarterly reset differently, one says the last Monday of March, June, September and December, another the first Monday of January, April, July and October. Rather than guess, read the punishment end date printed on your own shop.

## Appeals in 2026: earlier, and facts led

Since 20 April 2026, sellers can appeal penalty points in advance, up to seven days before the punishment starts. A wrong point can be contested before the shop loses its search visibility, not after. Appeals are submitted from Account Health in Seller Centre.

An appeal is a conversation about facts. If a metric looks wrong, you normally have to show what happened on the orders in question: which parcels were handed over, on which day, under which tracking number. ## The Monday routine that keeps points away

Ten minutes a week, in the same order:

1. Open Account Health and read the points, the punishment end date, and the areas flagged.
2. Check NFR and LSR for the week and note which orders moved them.
3. Hand over the parcels closest to a late handover first, before new orders.
4. Note the reason behind every cancellation and return from the week.
5. Clear unread buyer chats, because response time feeds the customer service side.
6. Appeal anything that looks wrong inside the pre appeal window, not after the punishment lands.

Boring on a good week, which is exactly when it is cheap.
