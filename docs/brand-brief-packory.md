# PACKORY — BRAND & WEBSITE CREATIVE BRIEF (REVISED FOR PACKORY)

Act as a world-class Senior Director of UI/UX, Brand Director, and CMO specializing in B2B SaaS, ecommerce technology, fulfillment operations, and fraud/evidence platforms.

Your task is to design a premium, modern, high-converting website for Packory — a hands-free, on-device packing verification platform for ecommerce sellers.

## WHAT PACKORY ACTUALLY IS

Packory turns any cheap warehouse Android phone/tablet into a fully automated packing verification camera:

1. An AWB / order barcode is detected automatically (no button, no screen touch).
2. Recording starts by itself. A UTC timestamp is burned into every frame.
3. The packer finishes, shows a thumbs-up — the clip stops automatically.
4. The clip saves instantly on the device, then syncs in the background to the seller's own private Google Drive (BYO storage — zero server video overhead), resuming on reconnect.
5. A masked evidence link is delivered to the buyer on WhatsApp automatically.
6. The buyer opens the link and sees their order being packed correctly — before it even ships.
7. (Roadmap) The buyer can reply with an unboxing video on the same link — a two-sided evidence chain: how it left, how it arrived.

### The core job

Beat parcel-swap and empty-box fraud claims. In Shopee, TikTok Shop, Lazada, and beyond, sellers lose refunds and disputes because they have no proof of what was packed. Packory is the proof.

### Positioning statement

Extended: Packory is the operating system for dispute-proof ecommerce packing — hands-free video evidence for every parcel, delivered to the buyer on WhatsApp before it ships.

==================================================
BRAND COLOR SYSTEM (KEEP — do not change)
==================================================

PRIMARY / FOUNDATION — Carbon Black #0F1417
Authority, trust, infrastructure, reliability, operational control. Use for ~50–60% of the visual system: hero background, navigation, footer, large premium sections, dashboard dark surfaces, major headings on light backgrounds, high-trust conversion areas. Avoid pure black #000000.

SECONDARY / BRAND COLOR — Royal Purple #6045F4
Brand recognition, intelligence, innovation, product actions, technology. Use ~15–20%: primary CTA buttons, active navigation, links, selected tabs, product actions, automation, important highlights, brand icon/logo symbol. White text on Royal Purple.

TERTIARY / SIGNAL COLOR — Neon Mint #53E6D4
Operational intelligence, live activity, synchronization, success, movement. Use ~5–10%. NOT a generic CTA color. Use for: LIVE status, synced, recording, verified, evidence sent, positive KPIs, live indicators, workflow movement, status labels, eyebrow labels. Carbon Black text on Mint.

FOURTH / NEUTRAL — Soft Gray #EBEBED
Clarity, whitespace, readability, calm. Use ~15–20%: light sections, feature cards, testimonials, integrations, comparison, forms, dividers. Carbon Black typography on Soft Gray.

CORE COLOR RULE:
PURPLE = BRAND + ACTION
MINT = SIGNAL + SUCCESS + LIVE DATA
BLACK = AUTHORITY + CONTROL
GRAY = CLARITY + WORKSPACE
Never randomly interchange these roles.
